    Skill advancement

      Checks

      How every roll works, and who sets the number.

      The roll

      d20roll+ RankSkill+ ModStatvs DCtarget

      Meet or beat the target and you succeed. Only players roll d20s. The GM never rolls against you; Mobs set the number you have to reach.

      A full Stat name (Strength) means the score. The short form (Str) means its Mod. Mods come from your Enhanced score.

      Core p. 58, Survival p. 12

      Difficulty

      Unopposed (climbing, swimming, endurance, most survival): 10 + 2×F = 12

      Opposed (a foe resists you, including social checks): 10 + foe’s Mod + F = 11 + Mod

      Stat Check (no Skill, usually resisting something): 10 + F = 11

      Your attacks target the Mob’s Evade: 10 + Dex Mod + F = 11 + Dex Mod

      The GM can set any number and tells you the result, not the target.

      Core p. 59, 82; Survival p. 13–14

      Unenhanced is your base plus Race, Class, potions and level-ups. Enhanced adds gear, Spells and Buffs.

      Core p. 57

      Advantage and Disadvantage

      Advantage: roll two d20s, keep the higher. Disadvantage: keep the lower.

      They never stack. One of each cancels out; whatever is left over wins (two Advantages and one Disadvantage still means Advantage).

      On Mobs: Advantage adds 5 to the Difficulty you face, Disadvantage takes 5 off. A Disadvantaged Mob attack gives you a free Evade Check; fail it and you can still spend an Action to Evade again.

      Core p. 60, Survival p. 14

      No Ranks in it?

      Attack or Utility Skill: you can try it with Disadvantage, adding your Stat Mod but no Ranks.

      Spells: not without Ranks, unless you cast from a scroll. Passive Skills: never untrained.

      Floors 1–2Note every Skill you try untrained. At the end of each 2-hour session, gain 1 Rank in one of them, then wipe the list. No saving them up.

      Floor 3+Untrained use no longer teaches Skills. Get them from gear, potions, a Guild (Rank 1, paid), your Race or Class.

      Core p. 58, Survival p. 12

      Teamwork

      Help (an Action, before the roll): add 1d2 to an ally’s non-combat Skill Check, or 1d6 if you have Ranks in that Skill.

      Intervene (Interrupt, after the roll): add 1d6 to an ally’s d20 Check. Made for turning a Near Miss into a hit.

      Party Skill Check: everyone rolls, no helping. Half or more succeed and the party succeeds. An Amazing Success flips one failure; a Major Fail flips one success.

      Nobody can help with a Stat Check.

      Core p. 59–60, 64, 82

      Trying again

      Out of combat, if retrying is possible and nothing is at stake, the GM may skip the roll.

      After a Major or Critical Fail you can’t try that Skill again in the same scene.

      Escalating checks list several targets (say 12, 17, 22). You get the best one you beat and everything below it.

      Core p. 60–61

      Your sheet, decoded

      Evade: d20 + Dex Mod + Evade Buffs. No Ranks.

      To hit: Skill Rank + Stat Mod. Damage: dice + Stat Mod from the weapon entry.

      DR: Armor + Buffs. Health Bar: 10 slots, each worth your Con Mod.

      Max Mana: your Enhanced Intelligence score (the score, not the Mod).

      Defaults: Move 20 ft, Step 10 ft. Humans start with 1 AI Favor and are Size 4 (Medium).

      Crawlers & Customization p. 14–16

      To work out a roll for you, use the check calculator.

      Combat round

      Mobs declare first, you react, then you strike back. No initiative.

      One round, five steps

      1. GMMobs declare

        Where they move, who they attack, and how.

      2. CrawlersReact

        Spend Actions on Interrupts: Evade, Intervene, Heal, Taunt, Catcher.

      3. GMMobs resolve

        Damage lands on anyone who didn’t Evade. Your DR applies. Debuffs from attacks land now.

      4. CrawlersAct

        Spend what’s left in any order, mixing turns freely.

      5. EveryoneClean up

        Ongoing damage and effects tick, Dying counts down, Actions reset.

      Core p. 80–86, Survival p. 32–37

      Your two Actions

      You get 2 Actions per round (a round is 10 seconds). Interrupts in step 2 come out of those same two.

      Every Action comes with a free 10 ft Step. Talking is free.

      You can wait for an ally to go first, but you can’t hold an Action into the next round.

      Keep tokens in view so the GM sees who has Actions left.

      Core p. 63–64

      Starting a fight

      The GM sets positions and terrain first.

      Surprise: if you catch foes off guard, each crawler gets 1 free Action before step 1. It happens once.

      Stealth success before combat: 1 non-Attack Action (an Attack with Advantage at Rank 5).

      Otherwise Mobs go first.

      Core p. 80, 199

      What Mobs get

      Ordinary Mobs: 1 Move and 1 other Action (usually an attack), spread across crawlers in reach.

      Elites and Bosses: 1 Action per crawler in the fight, even after some of you go down.

      Mobs die at 0% Health. If a Mob forces you to attack a party member, they get a free Evade.

      Core p. 81, 87

      Actions

      What one Action buys you, in step 2 and step 4.

      Interrupts (step 2, 1 Action each)

      Evade: one Evade Check per attack aimed at you, against that attack’s value. Against an area: half damage, or a quarter in the Splash zone. A Step won’t carry you out of a blast.

      Intervene: add 1d6 to another crawler’s d20 Check after they roll. Not on Stat Checks.

      Heal: cast a healing Spell or use a healing item before Mob damage lands. Also works as a normal Action.

      Core p. 81–82

      Protecting allies

      Taunt (Skill, Cha, Int-Opposed): after Mobs declare, pull one attack from a Mob within 30 ft of you onto yourself, more on higher degrees. You can then spend your other Action to Evade. Rank 5: free Evade against Taunted attacks.

      Catcher (Passive Skill): take a direct, non-area hit meant for an adjacent ally; Step 10 ft first if needed. You can’t Evade or block the hit you catch.

      Core p. 82, 189, 200

      On your turn (step 4)

      Attack: weapon, fists or Spell against Evade. Swapping to a Hotlist weapon is part of the attack.

      Cast a Spell: from your Hotlist, paying Mana.

      Make a Check: use a Skill, like First Aid. Checks the GM calls for mid-fight usually cost nothing.

      Move: Move value (20 ft) plus your Step.

      Use a Hotlist Item or Retrieve one from Inventory (you can’t use it in that same Action).

      Core p. 64, 82–86

      Clever plays

      Call a Play: name an Action for an ally, roll 2d6 and keep the higher. Add it to their Check when they do it; if they’d have succeeded anyway, it goes on damage instead.

      Look for Clues (Boss fights only): Unopposed Perception or Investigation. Learn a weakness, resistance, attack or terrain trick; more on Amazing. Pick what to study.

      Core p. 85–86

      Costs nothing

      • Talking
      • A 10 ft Step with each Action
      • Dropping prone
      • Swapping weapons as part of an Attack
      • Most External Buffs when they trigger
      • A Skill Check the GM calls for mid-fight

      Core p. 64, 69, 85, 95

      Attacks

      Hitting things, hurting things, and blowing things up.

      To hit

      d20 + Attack Skill Rank + Stat Mod against the target’s Evade: 11 + its Dex Mod (10 + Dex Mod + F). Some stat blocks add more.

      Melee uses Str. Physical ranged uses Dex. Spells use Int. The Skill entry has the final say (Pugilism, for one, hits with Dex).

      Core p. 82–83

      Range

      Melee reaches 5 ft. Reach reaches 10 ft, not through occupied spaces (a diagonal past one open corner is fine).

      Ranged weapons and Spells have no penalty inside their listed range and automatically fail beyond it.

      Ranged attacks and ranged Spells made inside an enemy’s melee reach roll with Disadvantage.

      Core p. 82–83

      Damage

      Base dice + Stat Mod (per the entry) + Rank damage die (if the entry grants one) + bonuses. The target’s DR comes off after.

      Critical Hit: double the base dice, including upgrade dice that say “base damage”. The Rank damage die is not doubled.

      Amazing Success: +F, which is +1 right now.

      Core p. 79, 85, 176

      Hand-to-hand

      Unarmed Combat: every crawler has it. 1d4 + Str Bludgeoning, no Damage Effects.

      Skills like Pugilism, Wrasslin’ and Foot Soldier let you pick one Damage Effect (Iron Punch, Powerful Strike, Choke Out…) before you roll. It applies on a hit and still goes on cooldown on a miss.

      Mark either the Attack or the Damage Effect for advancement, not both. Skip Damage Effects for most of a 3+ round fight to earn the Skill’s AI Favor.

      Core p. 179–180

      Area attacks

      Roll once against one target in range. On a hit, roll damage once and it hits everyone in the area, allies included.

      Blast: out from the target point. Burst: out from you. Line: 5 ft wide. Cone: 60° arc. +Splash: an outer ring taking half.

      Evading halves it; Evading in the Splash leaves a quarter. Round down.

      Most misses do nothing, but thrown things and Fireball still land: roll 1d8 for direction, and they drift as many feet as you missed by.

      Core p. 83–84, 200–201

      Situations

      Cover: attacks from beyond melee range have Disadvantage. Melee ignores cover.

      Darkness, bad weather, shifting ground: Disadvantage. High ground: Advantage.

      Unseen: Advantage attacking someone who can’t see you; Disadvantage against a target you can’t see.

      Held or prone (Take Down) targets: Advantage.

      Size: target 4+ sizes bigger, Advantage; 4+ smaller, Disadvantage (a big attacker who hits anyway in melee adds its size to damage).

      Core p. 84–85, 97

      Core p. 85

      Exploring

      Moving, seeing, hiding, and the things that kill you slowly.

      Movement

      Move Action: 20 ft, plus the Step, so 30 ft. Two Moves cover 60 ft.

      Split movement around your other Action. Pass through allies, never enemies. On a grid, diagonals cost the same (1 square is 5 ft).

      Drop prone: free, gives Take Down. Stand up: 10 ft of movement (your Step). Pick up an item: 10 ft.

      Forced movement: Push is away, Pull is toward, Slide is any direction.

      Core p. 69–70, 80

      Rough going

      Difficult terrain: every foot costs 2.

      Climbing, swimming, crawling: every foot costs 2 (3 in difficult terrain). Sheer walls or currents need an Unopposed Skill Check.

      Jumping: Unopposed Jumping Check to clear up to Rank + Str Mod feet. Costs normal Move.

      Anyone with a climb, swim or fly Move pays nothing extra.

      Core p. 69–70

      Light

      Bright: no penalty.

      Dim: Disadvantage on sight-based checks such as Perception and Investigation.

      Dark: Disadvantage wherever sight matters, and the ground counts as difficult terrain.

      A torch or the Torch Spell (1 Mana) gives 20 ft bright light, then 20 ft dim.

      Core p. 69, Crawlers & Customization p. 40

      Hiding and sneaking

      Hide in Shadows (Dex, Int-Opposed) while in cover, dim light or darkness. Behind something bigger than you: Advantage. Success makes you Invisible until you fail a Hide check, attack, or show yourself.

      Stealth (Dex, Int-Opposed) to move unseen. You must start out of every foe’s sight.

      Moving at a fast travel pace rules out hiding.

      Core p. 71, 76, 195, 199

      Hazards

      Falling: 1d6 Bludgeoning per 10 ft (max 20d6), and you land prone.

      Holding your breath: Con Mod rounds. After that, Drowning: 1d6+1 each round.

      No food for a 30-hour day: Survival Check or lose 3 slots and gain Fatigued until you eat.

      No water for a day: lose 3 slots and gain Fatigued until you drink.

      Core p. 76

      A day assumes 10 hours of travel. Each 2 hours past that: Unopposed Endurance or gain Fatigued.

      Core p. 70–71

      The clock

      A dungeon day is 30 hours. The recap show airs in saferooms every 30 hours.

      Floor collapse (usual timer, the GM can change it): Floor 1, 5 days; Floor 2, 6; Floor 3, 8; Floor 4, 10; Floor 5, 15. Yours: 5 days

      Cooldowns: once per round means not with both Actions. Once per day is per 30 hours. Once per X days and once per floor reset when you reach a new floor.

      Core p. 63, Survival p. 17

      Health

      Damage comes off in whole slots. So does healing.

      Taking damage, in order

      1. DR comes off first. Damage from Debuffs skips this step.
      2. Resistance to that type halves what’s left.
      3. Vulnerability doubles it.
      4. Immunity makes it zero, along with any Debuff riding on it.
      5. Lose one slot for every full slot value left over. Less than one slot’s worth is ignored.

      Mixed damage types split evenly, and each part checks its own resistances.

      Example: slots of 4, 22 damage. Five slots go (20), the last 2 are ignored.

      The Health and Mana tracker does this for you.

      Core p. 92–94

      Zero and Dying

      At 0% you can’t act until healed. Healed during step 4, you can still use any Actions you had left.

      Losing your last slot gives the Dying Debuff: a countdown of Con Mod rounds. It drops by 1 at each Clean Up and each time you’d take damage. Heal 1 slot (10%) to stop it.

      While Dying: no Actions, no moving, no talking, no HUD.

      If Debuff damage would drop you to 0%, roll a Con Stat Check against 11 at Clean Up (free). Success ends that Debuff and skips the damage.

      Core p. 94, 96–97

      Healing and rest

      Heal Spell: every crawler has it. 2 Mana, an Interrupt, self only, heals 2 slots.

      Each calm hour outside combat: 1 slot and 5 Mana.

      Short rest (2 h): 5 slots and half your max Mana, rounded down.

      Long rest (8 h): full Health and Mana, clears all Fatigued.

      Full day (30 h): full recovery, and the only cure for a Long-Term Major Injury.

      Rest means a saferoom or truly relaxing. Healing never goes past 100%.

      Core p. 94–95, Crawlers & Customization p. 15

      Injuries

      Minor Injury: −2 to all Checks until a short rest. A second one becomes Long-Term (needs a long rest).

      Major Injury: −5 to all Checks until a long rest. A second one becomes Long-Term (needs a full day).

      You get them after combat from a Major Fail (Minor) or natural 1 (Major) on an Evade.

      First Aid Rank 5: spend 5 minutes to also cure a Minor Injury.

      Core p. 80, 96, 195

      Damage types

      Common: Bludgeoning, Fire, Piercing, Poison, Slashing.

      Uncommon: Acid, Electric, Force, Holy, Ice, Necrotic, Psychic, Sonic.

      Types only matter for Resistance, Vulnerability and Immunity.

      Core p. 93

      Magic & gear

      Mana, Spells, potions, and what fits where.

      Casting Spells

      A Spell has to be in your Hotlist to cast it in combat or under pressure. Casting takes an Action.

      Attack Spells: d20 + Spell Rank + Int Mod against Evade; damage is base + Int.

      Passive (non-Attack) Spells just happen. No roll, and they don’t earn advancement marks.

      Muted means no casting. Ranged Spells inside melee reach take Disadvantage.

      Floor 3+Spells Favored by a Class cost everyone else 1 extra Mana.

      Core p. 85, 202; Crawlers & Customization p. 36

      Mana

      Max Mana equals your Enhanced Intelligence score. Update it when Intelligence changes.

      Get it back by resting (see Health), Mana Potions (full), Mana Toast in some saferooms, or a Replenish Buff (Int Mana every 10 minutes).

      Core p. 94, 96; Crawlers & Customization p. 15

      Scrolls, books, potions

      Scroll: casts its Spell once at the Rank written on it, no Mana, then crumbles. Works even without Ranks.

      Spellbook or tome: read it to learn the Spell at a set Rank (random is 1d6−1, minimum 1).

      Potion: works from the Hotlist without physically drinking, if you’re conscious. An Action in combat; healing potions can be an Interrupt. One potion every other round at most, or Potion Sickness (Poisoned for 15 seconds).

      Core p. 99

      Hotlist and Inventory

      Hotlist: 10 slots, up to 999 of the same item per slot. Anything there can appear in your hand or at your feet as part of using it.

      Inventory: weightless, but gives no bonuses. Pulling something out is a Retrieve Action; from Inventory to an attack takes 2 Actions.

      You must lift an item for 4 seconds to stash it (you lift Strength × 15 lb). No living things (they die), loose liquids, or explosives within 3 seconds of going off.

      Core p. 98; Crawlers & Customization p. 15

      Gear slots

      Head, Torso, Arms, Legs, Feet: one item each.

      Hands: one item per hand plus a pair of gloves. A shield takes a hand; Two-Handed takes both.

      Accessories: up to 10 (one belt, one cape).

      Only equipped gear gives bonuses.

      Core p. 98–99

      Buffs

      Internal Buffs are always on while you have the source, like Stat boosts or Invisible.

      External Buffs fire on a trigger, and you choose whether to use them (handy with cooldowns). Examples: Damage Reflection, Resistances, Safe Fall.

      Rule of Three: only 3 External Buffs active. Pick them at the start of the day; swap during a short rest.

      Core p. 95–96

      Junk, loot and gold

      Misc. Junk: spend 1 to find any small household item in your Inventory (not food or water). Merchants pay 1 gold each.

      Dead Mobs drop 1d6 Junk each plus gold by floor. Yours: no gold

      Bosses always drop a map. Open loot boxes in a saferoom; unopened boxes can’t be taken from a body.

      Crafting: Fabricate (or Engineering for moving parts), Unopposed. Takes size × size hours.

      Floor 4+Magic crafting needs a crafting table in a personal space.

      Core p. 88, 99, 215–222

      Growth & fame

      Levels, Skill Ranks, AI Favor and the audience.

      Gaining levels

      Every 2 hours of play: +1 level for everyone.

      Quest done: at least +1.

      Boss kill (each party member): Neighborhood 1, Borough 2, City 3, Province 4, Country 5, Floor 6.

      Killing a crawler: 1d6 levels, plus the level gap if they outranked you (max 15), minus it if you outranked them (min 1). It also earns a Player Killer skull.

      Floor 3+Each level gives 3 Stat points, spent in a saferoom. Levels from Floors 1–2 count once you arrive.

      Core p. 87–88, 169–170

      Raising Skills

      Each time you roll a Skill, pass or fail, tick its box.

      Advancement check: 1d20 equal to or over the current Rank gives +1 Rank.

      When: Rank 4 or lower, every 2 hours of play. Rank 5+, at the end of each floor. Then erase all ticks.

      Passive Skills don’t tick; they rise through magic, training, Race or Class. Levels never raise Skills.

      Cap: Rank 15 on Floors 1–5. Upgrades: Ranks 5, 10 and 15. Working on your floor: Rank 5 only

      Core p. 169, 173; Crawlers & Customization p. 20, 42

      Grinding

      Declare hours against weak Mobs. Up to 5 a day is safe; each extra hour needs an Unopposed Endurance Check or it’s wasted and you gain Fatigued.

      Put hours into Skills. When a Skill has hours equal to its Rank, roll its advancement check. Same Skill once a day.

      Neighborhood Map: +1 hour on a 5+ hour grind. Borough Field Guide: +2.

      Total grind hours equal to your level gives a level (the lowest-level crawler gets 1d2).

      Core p. 170–171, 215–216

      AI Favor

      Spend 1 to: reroll a d20, once per Check, before the GM announces the result (never a natural 1). Or, once per round, take an extra non-Attack Action.

      Earn it: heroics, surviving something stupid, sticking it to the showrunners, amusing the AI, and using an attack marked with AI Favor for most of a fight of 3+ rounds.

      Humans start with 1.

      Core p. 61, 175; Crawlers & Customization p. 16

      Popularity

      +1, once per session: a natural 20 or 1 in a fight or life-or-death check; a courageous act (Catcher alone doesn’t count); an original solution that works.

      +1 for making the daily recap, and at floor’s end for a cute pet moment, a memorable catchphrase, or Charisma of 5 or more (5 × F).

      −1: dodging a fight or being boring or gross (per session). At floor’s end: a big d10+ attack as your main move, Charisma of 2 or less (2 × F), taking the stairs 30+ hours early, or grinding 15+ of the final 30 hours.

      Core p. 279–280

      Playing to the cameras

      Once per 2-hour session, before an important, dramatic Check, announce who you’re playing to. You roll it with Disadvantage.
